About Irene

A Creative Identity Architect, not a logo shop.

Irene Deborah started Forge Visuelle on a simple frustration: too many brands look interchangeable. Same fonts, same palettes, same safe choices — identities that could belong to anyone, which means they belong to no one.

She works as an architect, not a decorator. Before any visual is touched, she maps the structure underneath — what the brand stands for, who it's for, and what it needs people to feel the moment they encounter it.

The name says it plainly. A forge takes raw material and, through heat, pressure, and deliberate craft, shapes it into something strong and singular. That's the process Irene brings to every brand she builds: nothing mass-produced, nothing left to template.
